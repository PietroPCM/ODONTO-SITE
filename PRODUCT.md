## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (no framework, no build), in this repo, deployed as static files on Vercel (`vercel.json`). Chosen by the user on 2026-10-16 over a new Next.js project; the live site at site-dra-aline.vercel.app is a separate Next.js build whose source is not available.

## Users

Parents and guardians in Cascavel (PR) and region looking for a pediatric dentist they can trust with their child. Most arrive on a phone from Instagram (@alinesanfelice) or Google Maps, and their job is to decide, quickly, whether this professional is right for their child and then start a WhatsApp conversation. Children are the patients, not the readers.

Typical situations: first visit; a child afraid of the dentist or with a bad previous experience; a child who has more difficulty being treated (families of atypical/TEA children appear in the reviews); questions about sedation; prevention from the first teeth.

## Product Purpose

Personal-brand site for Dra. Aline Sanfelice, Odontopediatria Avançada. It must make parents trust her within seconds and convert interest into a WhatsApp contact, without hard selling.

## Positioning

The brand is the professional herself, not a generic clinic. Families describe her patience, delicacy, care and the playful (lúdico) way the visit is conducted, including procedures with dental sedation and children who struggle with dental care. Her clinic is newly built ("O novo consultório está lindo", review) with a pastel arched interior that echoes her rainbow logo.

## Product Principles

1. Trust before persuasion: answer the parent's questions in the order they ask them; WhatsApp is the natural end of each answer, not a shout.
2. Her, not a clinic: every section should only be true of Dra. Aline.
3. Proof is quoted, never invented: real reviews, real photos, real address.
4. Responsible about clinical claims: sedation and difficult cases are always framed as "after evaluation".
5. Phone first: Instagram → site → WhatsApp is the main path.
